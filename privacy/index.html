Peku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-14

What Peku does

Peku is an SSH terminal client for iPhone and iPad. It connects directly to computers you choose. Peku does not operate a hosted terminal service, require a Peku account, include advertising, or send usage analytics to its developer.

Information on your device

Peku stores connection settings, saved hosts, recent workspace information and terminal previews on your device. Terminal preferences and shortcut settings are also stored locally. Authentication credentials, including SSH private keys, are stored in the device's Keychain. Device backups and migration are subject to your Apple settings and the protection applied to each stored item.

Connections and terminal content

Pairing information is exchanged with the computer you choose over HTTPS. Terminal commands, text and authentication information needed for SSH are sent to that computer through the SSH connection. Terminal output is received from that computer. Peku verifies the pairing certificate fingerprint and the SSH host key. The developer does not receive your terminal content or authentication credentials through these connections. Your computer, network services and tools you run on it may process information under their own policies.

Camera, photos and clipboard

Camera access is requested to scan pairing QR codes. Peku uses the system photo picker to access images you select. Selected images are converted on your device and uploaded over SFTP to the connected computer when you request an upload; their file paths are inserted into the terminal. Uploaded files remain on your computer until you remove them. Clipboard content is used when you invoke paste, and selected terminal text is written to the clipboard when you invoke Copy. Peku does not send these items to the developer.

Local network and remote access

Local network permission enables connections to computers on your network. You can also connect through an existing Tailscale network configured separately on your devices. Peku does not provide or operate that network service.

Diagnostics and support

Peku writes operational errors to Apple's system logging facilities. It does not include an automatic developer-operated log upload service. If you contact support, the developer receives the contact details and information you choose to send, uses them to respond and investigate the issue, and retains them only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Avoid sending passwords, private keys or sensitive terminal content. Apple's diagnostic services and your email provider operate under their own policies.

Your choices

You can revoke camera and local network permissions in iOS Settings, remove saved hosts and shortcuts in Peku, and remove images uploaded to your computer from that computer. Removing a saved host removes its stored credentials from Peku's Keychain service. Deleting the app does not guarantee that all Keychain items or device backups are removed. Contact support for questions about information you have provided in a support request.

Support website hosting

Peku's support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement. These websites do not include developer-operated analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
